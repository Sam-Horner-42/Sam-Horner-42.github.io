Blasteroids.

An endless space shoot 'em up for the PICO-8 fantasy console. Dodge asteroid fields, blast them apart and collect gems while chasing a high score. I made every sprite and every piece of music myself.

Overview

No win screen. Just you, your ship and an endless asteroid field.

Blasteroids is built for PICO-8, a fantasy console with tight, deliberate limits: a 128×128 screen, a 16-colour palette and a small cap on code size. Working inside those limits means every sprite, sound and line of code has to earn its place.

There is no winning condition. The goal is to survive as long as possible and beat your best score.

Highlights

  • Original pixel art

    I drew every sprite, from the player ship to the asteroids and gems, within PICO-8's fixed palette.

  • Original soundtrack

    I composed all music and sound effects in PICO-8's built-in tracker.

  • Endless score attack

    Asteroid fields and gem pickups keep runs replayable and competitive.

  • Three weapons

    A rapid-fire blaster is backed by a limited stock of rockets and a laser pickup for when things get crowded.

How it was made

I handled the game design, all visual assets and all audio. I used AI assistance to help write the Lua gameplay code, and I integrated, tested and tuned that code within PICO-8's constraints.

The game proved too easy with 10 hearts so I lowered the health and added the shield mechanic. The sound effects and music took weeks of fine tuning.
